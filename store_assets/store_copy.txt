Elevate your push-up training with The Amazing Push Up!

100% Free! Support independent development:
Buy Me a Coffee: https://buymeacoffee.com/yair.garmin

Tired of manually tapping your watch or losing count mid-set? The Amazing Push Up uses smart 100 Hz motion processing to count your reps automatically and accurately live on your wrist. Whether you're knocking out quick sets throughout the day or full calisthenics workouts, every single rep counts.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

KEY FEATURES:

1. AUTOMATIC & ACCURATE REP COUNTING
Smart motion detection automatically counts every push-up repetition from top plank to bottom lockout and ignores floor setup and dismount movements—just get into plank position and start pushing.

2. QUICK SETS OR MULTI-SET WORKOUTS
• Quick Set: Drop down for a single standalone set anytime—ideal for daily volume and grease-the-groove training.
• Multi-Set Workout: Structured sessions with dedicated rest timers between sets.

3. TRACK PROGRESS & BEAT YOUR RECORDS
Track your daily and 7-day volume to build consistency, and chase your all-time Personal Records (Best Set, Best Day, Best Week) with celebratory gold screens and vibration alerts!

4. WIDGET GLANCE & GARMIN CONNECT SYNC
Check daily/weekly totals right from your watch's glance carousel, and sync all sessions directly to Garmin Connect as Strength Training activities with developer rep fields.

5. 100% FREE TO USE
No paywalls, no subscriptions, and no trial expirations.
